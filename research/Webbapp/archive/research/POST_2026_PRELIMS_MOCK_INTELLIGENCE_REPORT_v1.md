# Post-2026 Prelims Mock Intelligence — 2026–27

## Scope
Only material publicly documented after the 24 May 2026 UPSC CSE Preliminary Examination is included in this inventory.

## What this layer is for
This is a **mock-design intelligence layer**, not a claim that commercial mock questions reproduce UPSC setter behaviour. It captures publicly stated test architecture, release timing, current-affairs integration, question volume and simulation structure.

## Research rule
Paid/access-controlled question banks are not treated as if their unseen questions were available. Where only the program structure is public, only structure is recorded.

## Key observed design signals
1. Multiple 2027 series moved to a multi-stage model: sectional/foundation → applied/comprehensive → simulation.
2. Current affairs is increasingly integrated rather than isolated: ForumIAS explicitly states 20–25 CA questions per full test; PMF IAS runs a separate CA 360° stream and then integrates static + CA in FLTs.
3. Full-length simulations are increasingly positioned as a separate late-stage layer.
4. Large commercial platforms are increasing test volume, but volume itself is not evidence of UPSC-style fidelity.
5. Question-level mock data must be separately audited for statement architecture, source depth, elimination burden, novelty and PYQ similarity before entering the training corpus.
6. Public/open mocks are especially useful because they can be audited without relying on inaccessible paid content.

## Guardrail
Do not use commercial claims such as “most probable,” “direct hit,” or predicted-question language as ground truth. Treat them as provider claims unless independently demonstrated.
