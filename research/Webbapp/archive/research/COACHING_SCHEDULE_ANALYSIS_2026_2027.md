# Coaching Schedule Intelligence 2026–2027

## Sources analysed
PMF IAS 2026; VisionIAS 2026; VisionIAS 2027; ForumIAS PTS 2027.

## PMF IAS 2026
54 GS + 6 CSAT. GS architecture: 20 sectional, 8 advanced, 16 current-affairs, 10 comprehensive. Sectional allocation: Ancient 2, Medieval 1, Modern 3, Art & Culture 1, Polity 4, Geography 3, Environment 2, Economy 3, Static Full 1. Advanced: Polity, Ancient+Medieval, Economy, Modern, Agriculture, Geography, Environment, S&T (one each). The schedule explicitly separates Saturday current-affairs tests from Sunday sectionals.

## VisionIAS 2026/2027
Both documents use 35 tests: 8 Fundamental + 17 Applied + 10 Full-Length. They describe personalized Sandhan tests, large question repository, static/dynamic and macro/micro analytics, QRM and current-affairs integration. Exact per-subject question counts are not stated in the schedules and will not be fabricated.

## ForumIAS 2027
65 tests: Level 1 = 23 GS sectional + 7 CSAT + 1 GS comprehensive revision; Level 2 = 6 GS subject-comprehensive + 4 CSAT; Level 3 = 10 GS simulators + 9 CSAT simulators; 5 quarterly current-affairs tests. Level 1 GS allocation: Polity 4, Economy 4, Modern History 3, Geography 3, Environment 3, Art & Culture 2, Ancient 1, Medieval 1, General Science 1, Agriculture+S&T 1. Level 2 clusters: Polity; Economy; History; Geography+Environment; Art+Culture+S&T+Agriculture; Economic Survey+Budget+IR.

## Cross-provider learning
The common architecture is foundation/sectional -> applied/integrated -> full-length simulation. Current affairs is treated both as a standalone layer and as a modifier inside static subjects. Coaching source lists are useful for micro-topic and source-family mapping, but cannot be treated as UPSC prediction evidence. The final engine will use coaching schedules as a secondary prior after PYQ evidence.

## Error-learning hypotheses
Once actual coaching mock papers are uploaded, test for: source-driven niche facts; recency bias; PYQ-weight drift; excessive direct recall; insufficient statement/elimination structure; weak cross-subject integration; difficulty inflated by obscurity; and full-length papers that concatenate sectional style instead of being independently calibrated. These are hypotheses, not claims about the providers.

## Final calibration rule
PYQ corpus + official 2026 paper grammar + trend shift + coaching schedule coverage + coaching question-level analysis + current-affairs linkage + source provenance + difficulty/format calibration + duplicate control.
