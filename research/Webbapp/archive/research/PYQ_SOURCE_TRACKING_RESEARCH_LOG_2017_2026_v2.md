# Source Tracking Research Log — 2017–2026 — v2

## Research principle
The official UPSC paper is the source-of-record for the question itself. It does NOT disclose the setter's exact source.

For a PYQ from year Y:
- Original/primary source date must be <= Y.
- Proposition-level evidence must be matched.
- A later coaching article is retrospective corroboration only.
- A coaching statement such as "from NCERT" is not primary verification.
- No source is promoted merely because it makes the question solvable.

## Current web research
Official UPSC archive coverage for 2017–2025 was verified through the UPSC previous-question-paper archive. The archive explicitly lists Civil Services Preliminary GS-I papers for the relevant years. 2026 has a dedicated UPSC examination page showing GS-I upload on 25 May 2026.

PIB's 2026 release explicitly states that references/answers may be traced to standard textbooks, journals, government websites, government press releases and reputed newspapers. This is a general source-policy statement, not question-level provenance.

Secondary research sources were collected year-wise for transcription and analytical cross-checking. They are never automatically treated as original source evidence.

## 2026 special layer
Legacy IAS provides a complete 100-question source mapping. Its "primary source" labels are treated as SECONDARY_CLAIM_ONLY until the underlying dated institutional source is proposition-matched. This prevents a common provenance error.

## Completeness
The master ledger contains exactly 1,000 nominal records: 100 each for 2017–2026.

## What remains before a record can become PRIMARY_VERIFIED
1. Identify exact proposition tested.
2. Find original institutional/book/document source.
3. Confirm publication/document date <= PYQ year.
4. Match the proposition, not merely the topic.
5. Record exact source title/organization/date/URL.
6. Record whether the question is static, current-linked, hybrid, or source-dependent.
7. Independently audit answer/key status.
8. Retain ambiguity/contradiction flags rather than forcing a source.
