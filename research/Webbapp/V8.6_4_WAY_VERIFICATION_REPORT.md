## Data/architecture limitations
1. AI portal memory is persistent in browser-local storage with backup/restore. Durable cross-device/server persistence is configuration-dependent; the existing Vercel  path is not by itself a guaranteed durable database without an external durable store such as D1/DB.
2. The startup passkey gate is WebAuthn device/browser protection. It is not a server-side multi-user authentication system.
3. The 1,300-PYQ corpus is indexed and retrieved on demand for AI rather than sending all raw questions in every request, to keep prompts performant and within model context limits.
4. Mains trend figures are transcribed from the user-supplied screenshots. Separate Ancient/Medieval/Modern Mains rows were not present in the supplied source, so no artificial split was generated.
